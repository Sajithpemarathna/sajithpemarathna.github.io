E-Commerce Marketplace Performance Analytics

PostgreSQL, Python, SQL, Tableau

A full analytics stack over 99,441 orders worth $13.6M from the Olist Brazilian marketplace, built raw to staging to dimensional model in PostgreSQL and surfaced through two Tableau dashboards: an executive view of revenue and customers, and an operational view of sellers and delivery performance.

  • A 3.12% repeat rate reframes the business.96,096 customers placed 99,441 orders at an average value of $136.70. This behaves like a marketplace of one-time buyers rather than a loyalty-driven store, which changes where acquisition and retention spend belongs.
  • Late delivery runs at 6.57%, but the tail is the problem.Average delivery is 12.5 days and most orders land between 7 and 15. The distribution carries a long right tail past 40 to 60 days, and both freight cost and distance rise along it.
  • Revenue concentrates hard on a few sellers.Average revenue across 3,095 sellers is $4,391, while the leading seller turns over roughly $229k. Seller churn at the top is a materially different risk from churn in the tail.
  • Demand is geographically concentrated.São Paulo accounts for around 41.7k customers, Rio de Janeiro 12.9k and Minas Gerais 11.6k. Three states carry the majority of the customer base, which is also where freight spend is heaviest.
How it is built Python ingestion loads the source files into a raw PostgreSQL schema. SQL staging handles type correction, trimming, deduplication and key normalisation. The analytics layer builds dim_customers, dim_products, dim_sellers and dim_date alongside fct_orders and fct_order_items, with a dedicated BI view feeding each dashboard.
